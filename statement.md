Problem Statement

Managing personal finances manually is error-prone and tedious. Many individuals struggle to keep track of where their money goes, leading to poor financial health. There is a need for a simple, digital solution to record and categorize financial transactions efficiently.

Scope

The project focuses on a desktop-based console application for individual users. It covers the core aspects of financial tracking:

Recording inflows (Income) and outflows (Expenses).

Storing data permanently so records are not lost.

Providing instant feedback on financial standing (Net Balance).

Target Users

Students managing monthly allowances.

Freelancers tracking gig payments.

Anyone wishing to maintain a simple digital ledger of their expenses.

High-Level Features

Security: User-specific data isolation via login.

CRUD Operations: Create and Read financial records.

Analytics: Mathematical aggregation of data to show net totals.

Persistence: File-based database system.
