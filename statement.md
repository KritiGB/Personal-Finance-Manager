## Problem Statement
Managing personal finances manually is error-prone and tedious. Many individuals struggle to keep track of where their money goes, leading to poor financial health. There is a need for a simple, digital solution to record and categorize financial transactions efficiently.

## Scope
The project focuses on a desktop-based console application for individual users. It covers the core aspects of financial tracking:
- **Recording inflows:** Tracking Income.
- **Recording outflows:** Tracking Expenses.
- **Data Persistence:** Storing data permanently so records are not lost.
- **Instant Feedback:** Providing real-time calculation of financial standing (Net Balance).

## Target Users
* **Students:** Managing monthly allowances.
* **Freelancers:** Tracking gig payments.
* **General Public:** Anyone wishing to maintain a simple digital ledger of their expenses.

## High-Level Features
1. **Security:** User-specific data isolation via login.
2. **CRUD Operations:** Create and Read financial records.
3. **Analytics:** Mathematical aggregation of data to show net totals.
4. **Persistence:** File-based database system.
